Privacy

We store your email address, the domains you add, and the client names and emails you choose to enter. We use them only to check those domains and send you alerts and reports.

Data is stored with Supabase. Emails are sent through Resend. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, and we never see your card details.

We never sell or share your data. Delete a domain at any time from the dashboard. To delete your account and everything in it, email us and we will do it within 7 days.
