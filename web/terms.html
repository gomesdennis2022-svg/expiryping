ExpiryPing checks domains once a day and sends alerts on a best-effort basis. Registries, networks and email delivery can fail, so ExpiryPing is a safety net, not a replacement for renewing your domains and certificates.

We are not liable for losses from a missed or late alert. Our total liability is limited to what you paid us in the last 12 months.

Subscriptions renew monthly or yearly until cancelled. Cancel any time and keep your plan until the end of the paid period. Refunds within 14 days of a first payment, on request.

Only add domains you manage or have permission to monitor. Don't use ExpiryPing to send email to people who haven't agreed to receive it.
